From the painting

Each print is a reproduction of Kimberly’s original oil painting. Saatchi Art handles production and delivery, so you can choose your print online without arranging a custom order.

Is this the original painting?

No. These are open-edition reproductions. The original is a one-of-a-kind oil on canvas, 18 × 24 inches, created in 2023. View the original.

What happens after I order?

Complete your order through Saatchi Art. It produces and ships the print directly to you. Check its checkout for the estimated delivery date, shipping charges and applicable return terms before purchase.

Can I choose a frame?

Available framing and presentation options depend on the material and size. Choose those on Saatchi Art; the prices above do not include an optional frame. For paper prints, check the listed image size and paper border before choosing a frame.

Who can help me choose?

Email Kimberly about the artwork. Saatchi Art can help with ordering, delivery and its print options.
