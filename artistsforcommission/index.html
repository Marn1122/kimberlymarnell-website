Good to know

  • Extra people or animals: +25% of the base price each.
  • Need it sooner? Rush moves your order to the front for +25%.
  • Payment: orders under $500 are paid in full; $500 and over start with a 50% deposit, or pay in full.
  • Shipping: free and fully insured in the US up to 24 × 36 in. Larger sizes ship crated (+$175) or rolled in a tube for free.
  • Outside the US: shipping is quoted separately. Sales tax is added where required.
