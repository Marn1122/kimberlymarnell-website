Questions.

Does it cost anything to join?

No. There is no sign-up fee, monthly fee or listing fee. We take 10% only when you are paid for a commission.

When is the fee taken?

Automatically, from each payment the client makes through the site, including deposits and final payments. You never get a separate bill.

How do I get paid?

Through Stripe. You connect your bank account once. When a client pays, Stripe splits the payment automatically: 90% goes to your Stripe account and pays out to your bank, and 10% goes to Artists for Commission. Nobody has to send you money by hand.

Who sets the prices?

You do. Choose a price per square inch or a flat price for each size, plus any extras like more figures or rush orders.

Who owns the artwork?

You keep the copyright to your work. The client owns the physical painting. Reproduction or commercial use is agreed separately between you and the client.

What kind of artists are you looking for?

Painters and illustrators who create portraits of pets, people, families or homes from photos, with a consistent, professional portfolio.

Can I still take commissions elsewhere?

Yes. You're not exclusive. We only ask that clients who find you here order through the site.
