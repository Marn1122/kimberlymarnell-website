A hand-painted portrait for the holidays.

A portrait of someone you love, painted from your photos, is a gift that outlasts the season. Here is how a commission with Kimberly works and what it costs, with real examples.

By Kimberly Marnell · Haven Art House

Kimberly paints commissioned portraits of people, pets and places in oil and acrylic, in the same style as her own work: real likeness, with flowers, sky and light worked into the painting. You send the photos; she paints an original that exists nowhere else.

What it costs

Commissions are priced by size, so you always know the cost before you start:

  • Pets, places and other subjects: $2.50 per square inch
  • People: $3.00 per square inch
  • Each additional figure: +25%
  • Rush delivery: +25%

How it works

Choose your subject and size

The commission page walks you through five short steps and shows your price as you go.

Send your photos

Clear, well-lit photos give the best result. Natural daylight and a camera at eye level work better than flash. Send several if you can; Kimberly can combine them.

Pay to begin

Commissions under $500 are paid in full. For $500 and over, you can pay a 50% deposit to begin or pay in full. Card and bank transfer are both accepted.

Delivery

Shipping is free and insured within the US for paintings up to 24 × 36 in. Larger works (30 × 40 in and up) can ship in a wooden crate for $175, or rolled in a tube at no charge.

Timing for a holiday gift

A painting needs time to be made and time to dry before it ships, so the earlier you start, the easier it is to have it in hand for the holidays. If your date is close, rush delivery is available for 25% more. Email Kimberly before you order to confirm the date you need.

If the gift is a surprise, ask for the photos you need without giving it away: a recent phone photo of the pet, or a favorite family picture, is usually enough to start.
