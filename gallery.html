What Blooms in the Dark

Kimberly Marnell

Flowers that bloom at night recur through Kimberly Marnell's work: growth that needs no audience and no ideal conditions, something that develops quietly in the dark and opens when the time is right. Sixteen paintings and drawings from 2022 to 2026, hung in an ocean-view gallery above the Pacific.
