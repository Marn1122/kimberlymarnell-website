    How your plan works.

    • No interest and no fees. You pay exactly the painting's price.
    • Your first payment reserves the painting. It is marked Reserved and taken off sale.
    • Payments 2 and 3 are due on the dates in your schedule. You'll get reminders, and you can pay early at any time.
    • After your final payment, Kimberly ships the painting with free insured US shipping and a signed certificate of authenticity.
    • Need to change a date or cancel? Email Kimberly before your next payment date and she'll work it out with you. Refunds follow the returns policy.
